Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026  ·  Applies to all SSN Tech LLC service packages

Before you publish: replace every highlighted placeholder, and check these windows match how you actually want to work — a refund policy you do not follow is worse than none at all.

The short version

We sell fixed-scope work with a fixed price and a named delivery date. If we have not started, you get everything back. If we have started, you pay for the work done. If we deliver something that does not match the agreed scope, we fix it — and if we cannot fix it, we refund it.

1. Cancelling before work begins

You may cancel any engagement for a full refund at any time before work begins. Work "begins" at the earlier of: the kickoff call, or our first written confirmation that we have started.

In practice this means you can almost always change your mind within the first day or two at no cost.

2. Cancelling after work begins

Once work has begun, the amount refundable depends on how far we have got:

StageWhat happens
Discovery / audit stage The deposit (50% of the package price) is retained to cover scheduled time and discovery already performed. Any balance paid is refunded.
Build in progress We invoice for work completed to date and refund the remainder. We will share what has been built so far.
After delivery and acceptance No refund — but the remedy process in section 4 applies.

3. Advisory and audit deliverables

Deliverables that consist of analysis, research or recommendations — such as the AI Readiness Audit & Roadmap — are non-refundable once the report has been delivered. The work is the report, and it cannot be returned. Before delivery, section 1 and 2 apply as normal.

4. If the work is not right

We would rather fix something than refund it, and you will usually prefer that too.

  1. Tell us within 7 days of delivery. Describe what does not match the agreed scope.
  2. We correct it at no charge. In-scope defects are fixed within [10] business days, or sooner where practical.
  3. If we cannot make it conform to the agreed scope after a reasonable attempt, we refund the portion of the fee attributable to the non-conforming deliverable.

This covers work that does not match what was agreed. It does not cover a change of mind about what was agreed, new requirements discovered after the fact, or results affected by changes you or a third party made to the system after handover.

5. What is never refundable

  • Third-party costs we paid on your behalf — domains, licences, API credits, subscriptions, paid plugins — once purchased.
  • Work completed and accepted, including where you later stop using it.
  • Delays caused by non-delivery on your side, such as access, approvals or materials not provided (see Terms, section 5).
  • Outcomes we never promised — a specific revenue figure, ranking, conversion rate or hours saved. Estimates we give are estimates.

6. How to request a refund

Email sales@ssntechusa.com from the address associated with the engagement, with your invoice number and a short description of the problem. We acknowledge within one business day.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days of approval. How quickly it then appears depends on your bank or card issuer, not on us.

7. Please talk to us before filing a chargeback

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, contact us first. We keep full delivery records — scope confirmations, commit history, walkthrough recordings and handover notes — and almost every billing question is resolved the same day.

Filing a chargeback for work delivered in line with the agreed scope, without first raising it with us, is a breach of our Terms of Service. We will contest such disputes with our delivery records and may suspend any services still running.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version that applies to your engagement is the one published when you accepted that engagement's scope.
